Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a guaranteed site visit? +

A guaranteed site visit means a prospective property buyer who has been rigorously run through our 5-point telephone verification framework (budget match, timeline clearance, specific location intent) physically shows up at your project site office or layout location at a pre-confirmed weekend or weekday slot. We track physical verification metrics with your site manager.

How is this different from a traditional lead gen agency? +

A standard lead gen agency uses generic forms to gather basic names and numbers, drops them into a spreadsheet, and leaves your sales team to chase them. We provide a completely hands-off Done-For-You service. We generate the interest, filter out tire-kickers through active phone outreach, confirm their location coordination, and only book them when they are ready to meet your on-site manager.

How does the 10-visit guarantee work over 45 days? +

We engineer specialized localized ad campaigns targeting high-yield investors and end-users. Our dedicated internal calling desk calls every single digital lead within minutes. If our comprehensive filtering systems fail to deliver 10 budget-qualified physical site walk-ins into your database ledger within our 45-day cycle, our performance guarantee ensures you get a full per-visit milestone refund for any booking we miss.

What is the investment structure and how does ad spend work? +

We charge a fixed, non-refundable upfront setup fee of ₹20,000 to cover landing page infrastructure, custom script development, and dialing desk calibration. The ongoing performance fee is tied strictly to milestone delivery. Paid traffic advertising budget is funded separately by the client directly to Meta to maintain clear algorithm tracking. Full commercial parameters and risk-reversed structures are customized during our secondary Advisory Session.
