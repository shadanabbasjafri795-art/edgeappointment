Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is appointment setting? +

Appointment setting means we handle the entire process of finding serious buyers, qualifying them, and booking them directly into your calendar as confirmed appointments. By the time someone appears in your schedule, they already know who you are, what property you're offering, and they're genuinely interested. You just show up and have the conversation.

How is this different from a lead gen agency? +

A lead gen agency gives you a list of names and numbers and leaves the rest to you. You still have to call, qualify, follow up, and deal with people who aren't serious. We handle all of that. You only receive confirmed, pre-qualified appointments — people who have been screened and are ready to speak with you about buying a property.

How do you guarantee 50 appointments? +

We run targeted Facebook and Instagram ads to attract buyers, qualify them through a screening process, and book only the serious ones into your calendar. Our guarantee is backed by a discrepancy refund — for every appointment we miss under the agreed target, you get a full per-appointment refund. You literally only pay for what we deliver.

What is the pricing and how does the refund work? +

We work on a pure pay-per-appointment basis — you only pay for appointments we actually deliver. Our pricing is discussed on the discovery call based on your specific market and requirements. The appointment fee is protected by our discrepancy guarantee — for every appointment we miss under the agreed target, we refund you that amount. Ad spend is separate and goes directly to the ad platform.

Who is this best suited for? +

This works best for real estate agents in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities who are actively selling properties and want a consistent flow of serious buyers without wasting time on price-sensitive leads. It's especially effective for agents who are ready to grow but are held back by inconsistent lead quality.

How quickly will I get my first appointment? +

Most clients see their first booked appointments within the first two weeks after onboarding. The first few days are spent on setup — understanding your property, target buyer, and campaign configuration. We go live shortly after and appointments start building from there.

Will I need to hire a sales team? +

No. Most agents start by handling appointments themselves. The appointments we deliver are already pre-qualified, so the conversations are more focused and less time-consuming than typical cold leads. As volume grows, you can expand your process if needed.

How do I get started? +

Click any of the booking buttons on this page. We'll schedule a free 15-minute discovery call where we understand your current situation — how many appointments you're getting, what your target buyer looks like, and where the core bottlenecks exist.
