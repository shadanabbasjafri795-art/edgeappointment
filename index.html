Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I get my first appointment? +

Most clients start seeing initial traction within the first few weeks after setup, depending on the offer, market demand, ad spend, and speed of communication. Faster follow-up and a strong offer usually lead to quicker results.

How do you guarantee 50 appointments? +

We focus on building a complete acquisition system designed to consistently generate qualified opportunities. This includes strategy, funnel optimization, outreach systems, lead generation, and continuous improvement throughout the campaign. Results depend on factors like offer quality, response speed, market fit, and sales process execution.

Who is this best suited for? +

This is best suited for real estate agents that already have a valuable service or offer and want a predictable system for generating leads and booking appointments. It works especially well for those are willing to grow.

Will I need to hire a sales team? +

No. Many agents start by handling appointments themselves. As volume increases, you can later expand your sales process if needed. It totally depends on you.

What makes your approach different? +

We focus on appointment booking systems instead of just marketing and lead gen. Every step is built around generating qualified conversations and booking appointments.

What exactly is appointment setting is? +

Appointment setting is when a dedicated team handles your entire outbound sales process. We find your ideal clients, reach out, have the conversations, qualify them, and book them into your calendar as confirmed calls. By the time someone lands in your calendar they already know who you are and they're genuinely interested. You just show up and close.

How quickly will i get my first appointment? +

Most clients see their first booked call within 14–18 days of onboarding. The first week is setup — ICP research, funnel strategy, and campaign configuration. We go live on day 8. Results build momentum over 30–60 days as we optimise targeting and messaging.

What's the difference between you and a VA doing outreach? +

A VA sends messages. We run a system. That means targeted list building, tested copy, multi-channel sequences, live qualification conversations, objection handling, and booking management — all optimised continuously based on data. A VA following a script gets ignored. A trained setter running a proven system books calls.

How do i get started? +

If we think we can help, we'll schedule a longer call to walk you through the full plan, expected results, and investment. If we don't think we're the right fit, we'll tell you honestly.
